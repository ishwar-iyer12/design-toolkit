How it works

Three steps between finished work and money in your account.

  1. Track as you go

    Start a timer from the menu bar or log hours afterwards. Tag anything outside the brief as a revision round and Tally keeps it separate.

  2. Send an invoice that looks like yours

    Your typeface, your colours, your grid. Hours, fixed fees and licences drop in as line items, already totalled and taxed.

  3. Get paid, without the awkward email

    Clients pay by card or bank transfer from the invoice itself. If they go quiet, Tally sends the reminder so you don't have to.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does Tally take a cut of my payments?

No. You pay the payment provider's standard processing fee on card payments and nothing on bank transfers. Tally charges only the plan price.

Can I import clients and old invoices?

Yes. Upload a CSV from your current tool or spreadsheet and map the columns. Past invoices come in as records, so your income history stays in one place.

What does my client see?

A web page with your branding, the line items and a pay button, plus a PDF attached to the email. They don't need an account.

Can I cancel Studio at any time?

Yes. You drop back to Starter at the end of the billing period and keep every invoice you've sent.
