How it is built

Three layers, locked in register.

You already think in layers. Tally keeps the parts of an invoice apart, so changing one never disturbs the others.

  1. Letterhead

    Set it once. Your studio name, your typefaces, your bank details and your terms. Upload the fonts you own as OTF or WOFF2 and Tally sets the whole sheet in them, so the invoice looks like the rest of the project.

  2. Line items

    Bill by the hour, by the day or by a fixed fee, and mix all three on one sheet. Revision rounds beyond the ones you quoted become their own line, with the round number on it.

  3. Total

    VAT, deposits already paid and the amount due. The client pays by card or bank transfer from the invoice itself. When the money arrives, Tally stamps the sheet and sends both of you a copy.

Questions

Asked before signing up.

Can I design the invoice myself?

Yes, within a grid. You choose the typefaces, the paper colour, the position of your mark and the column widths. Tally keeps the amounts aligned and the legal details present, so a layout change cannot lose the VAT number.

What happens when a client pays late?

On the Studio plan Tally sends a reminder 3, 7 and 14 days after the due date. You write the wording once. On the Solo plan the invoice is marked overdue and you decide when to chase.

Can I bill in a currency other than pounds?

Yes. Each client has a currency, and each invoice is issued in it. Your reports convert to your home currency at the rate on the day the invoice was paid.

Can I get my invoices out again?

Every invoice exports as a PDF and every year as a CSV file your accountant can open. If you close your account, the export stays available for 90 days.
