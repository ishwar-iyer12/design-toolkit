Questions

Do my clients need a Tally account?

No. They get a link to the invoice, where they can approve, pay and download a PDF.

Can I use my own fonts?

Yes. Upload any font files you hold a licence for and they are embedded in your invoices and PDFs.

How do payments reach me?

Card payments are paid out to your bank account. Bank transfers go to you directly, and you mark them as received.

I bill by the hour. Does Tally still work?

It does. Add hourly line items next to fixed ones. Tally is just built so that you do not have to.

Can I bring in my existing clients and invoices?

You can import clients and past invoices from a CSV file, so your numbering carries on where it left off.
