Six things designers do for free, and what Tally does about each.

None of this is new to you. It goes unbilled because adding it by hand, three weeks later, feels like starting an argument. Tally adds it when it happens, with the scope the client signed next to it.

What slips How it usually goes What Tally does
Extra revision rounds "Just one more small tweak" becomes round five. Counts rounds against the number in scope. Round three onwards is logged at your hourly rate and the client is told before you start.
Deposits You start work on a promise and chase the deposit in week two. Sends the deposit invoice with the proposal. The project shows as "Waiting for deposit" until it clears.
Usage licences The logo you licensed for packaging turns up on a billboard. Records media, territory and term on the invoice, and reminds you 30 days before a licence ends so you can renew it.
Kill fees The project is cancelled at 70% and you invoice for nothing. Works out the fee from the stage reached and the percentage in your terms.
Rush work A Friday evening request for Monday morning, at the normal rate. Applies your rush rate to any work logged inside the notice period you set.
Late payment You write the awkward email yourself, or you do not write it. Sends reminders on day 1, 7 and 14 after the due date, in your name, with the statutory interest shown.

Questions designers ask first.

Will my client see that I am counting revision rounds?

Yes, and that is the point. The proposal states how many rounds are included. When a request would start a paid round, the client gets a short note saying so and can go ahead or hold off.

I charge a fixed fee, not by the hour. Does this still work?

Yes. Set a flat price per extra round instead of an hourly rate. Deposits, licences and kill fees work the same way on fixed-fee projects.

I am not VAT registered. Can I turn VAT off?

Yes. VAT is off until you add a VAT number. If you register later, add the number and the date, and invoices from that date include it.

Can I take my invoices with me if I leave?

Yes. Download every invoice as a PDF and every record as a CSV from Settings, on either plan, at any time.
