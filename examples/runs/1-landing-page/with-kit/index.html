Sheet 2 of 6 · Line items

Does it bill the way design work is sold?

Design is not sold by the hour or the unit. It is sold in rounds, licences and deposits. General invoicing tools have a field for none of them.

Revision rounds

In a spreadsheet

You know round three happened. You are not sure you said it would cost extra, so you let it go.

In Tally

The quote said two rounds. Tally counts each round you send for feedback and adds the third to the invoice at the rate the client already agreed.

Usage licences

In a spreadsheet

Usage rights sit in paragraph six of a contract PDF that nobody opens again. The two years pass without a word.

In Tally

Each licence is a line with its media, territory and end date. Sixty days before it lapses, you and the client both get a renewal invoice in draft.

Deposits

In a spreadsheet

You subtract the 50% by hand, then work out the VAT again to check it still adds up.

In Tally

The deposit invoice and the final invoice are linked. The balance, the VAT and the date the deposit was paid fill themselves in.

Sheet 3 of 6 · Late payers

Who chases the client when the due date passes?

Tally does, from your address and in your words. You stay the designer they like working with.

  1. Day 0

    Invoice sent

    The client gets a link. No account, no login. They pay by card or bank transfer.

  2. Day 14

    Due today

    A short reminder goes out at 9am, with the pay link at the top.

  3. Day 21

    One week late

    The second reminder quotes the late clause from the quote they signed.

  4. Day 44

    Late fee added

    Thirty days past due, the 2% fee becomes a new line and the invoice is sent again.

You set the days and the wording once. Any reminder can be paused for a client you would rather phone.

Sheet 6 of 6 · Questions

What designers ask before they switch

Do my clients need a Tally account?

No. They get a link, see the invoice as a page and as a PDF, and pay by card or bank transfer. Their accounts team can download a copy without signing up for anything.

What if I quote a flat fee with unlimited revisions?

Set the rounds on the quote to unlimited and Tally stops counting. You can still log rounds for your own records. They will not reach the invoice.

Can the invoice look like my work, not like Tally?

On Studio, yes. Upload your typefaces and logo, and set margins and column widths. Tally keeps the figures in tabular numerals and the totals aligned, and embeds the fonts in the PDF.

What happens to my invoices if I stop paying?

Your account moves to the free plan. Every invoice you have sent stays readable, payable and exportable as CSV and PDF. Scheduled reminders after the first are paused.

Does Tally handle VAT?

It adds VAT at the rate you set for each client, shows your VAT number, and applies the reverse charge wording for business clients in the EU. It does not file your return. Your accountant gets a quarterly export. Questions: hello@tally.example
