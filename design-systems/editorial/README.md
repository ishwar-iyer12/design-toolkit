# Editorial

Pick this when people will read for more than a minute: articles, essays, documentation, changelogs, case studies.

Do not pick it for anything with a toolbar.

## What changes from base

**Type.** A serif for body and headings (Source Serif 4), a sans for captions, bylines and controls (Source Sans 3). Both are open fonts under the SIL Open Font Licence. They are not bundled here; load them yourself or let the fallbacks take over. Body is 19px on a 30px line.

**Measure.** Text columns are 66 characters wide, never under 45 or over 75. The page can be wide. The column cannot.

**Rhythm.** Everything vertical is a multiple of the 30px baseline or sits on the 4px grid. Paragraphs are separated by one line. A heading gets two lines above it and 16px below, so it belongs to the text that follows.

**Colour.** Warm off-white page, near-black text, and one oxblood accent used for links. Contrast is 16.11:1 for text, 6.55:1 for muted text and 9.00:1 for links.

**Effects.** No shadows at all, not even on floating layers. Use a 1px border.

## Rules for the agent

- Left-align body text. Never justify it, never centre it.
- Links are underlined. Colour alone does not mark a link.
- Headings use weight and size, not colour.
- Images and figures may break out wider than the measure. Text may not.
- Use real quotation marks and apostrophes. Hyphenate nothing automatically in headings.
- Pull quotes repeat text that exists in the article. If it is new information, it is a paragraph.
- Still five type sizes. Footnotes and captions share the caption size.
