# Showcase

Pick this when the page has to be remembered: a launch, a portfolio, a product home page where the first five seconds decide everything.

Do not pick it for a second page of the same site. One showcase page, then `marketing` or `base` for the rest.

## Why this variant exists

I ran the kit against an agent with no guidance, and the page with no guidance looked better. It had an idea taken from the product's world, a typeface with character, a second colour, and depth. The `marketing` variant had stripped all of that out and left something tidy and forgettable.

So this variant keeps the discipline (five sizes, the 4px grid, counted accents, designed states) and gives back the things that make a page worth looking at.

## What changes from base

**A concept comes first.** Before any token, write one sentence that names an idea from the product's own world. "A baker's order slips." "Air traffic strips." "A seed catalogue." Every visual choice after that answers to the sentence.

**Type with character.** `font.pairings` lists four open-licence pairs with the mood each suits. Pick by concept, or bring another open pair and say why. The display size is 88px, used once.

**A support colour.** One, used for at most two things, in addition to the accent's three. Change both colours to fit the concept. The defaults are deep green and peach.

**Depth.** Three shadow layers that grow with height, one hard offset shadow to use as a motif, and 3D transforms with the limits in `depth`. The signature object is large and rests in a 3D pose. Timid depth is worse than none.

**A signature animation.** One per page, built from the product, up to three beats of 1200ms. Rules are in the `motion` skill.

**Still no gradients and no glass.** They were not what made the other page good.

## Rules for the agent

- Write the concept sentence and put it in your report. If you cannot write one, ask what the product is like to use.
- The concept shows up in at least three places: the hero, one recurring motif, and the wording.
- Depth means something. A thing that is raised is on top of something, was picked up, or can be moved.
- The signature animation and the concept are the same idea. If they are not, one of them is wrong.
- Everything in `taste` still applies, including the smell test. This variant relaxes three rules (shadows, a second colour, 3D). It relaxes nothing else.
- Check the page with motion off and fonts failing to load. It must still look designed.
